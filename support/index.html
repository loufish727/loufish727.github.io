Help & support

Can't sign in?

For a password reset, open the app and choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. If you're still locked out, email us from the address you use for the app.

maintenance@lfes.app

Never send passwords or verification codes.

Already signed in?

Choose Report Issue to report a problem with the app. Check Messages for replies. If you don't see Report Issue, you can email support instead.

For a broken machine or a repair, submit a maintenance request to your facility instead.

Privacy & account deletion

Use the email address associated with your account so we can verify the request. You don't need to sign in. Deleting your sign-in is separate from deleting your company's shared maintenance records.
